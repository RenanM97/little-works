Teleprompter Privacy Policy

Effective October 15, 2026

The short version: Teleprompter does not collect any personal data. Your scripts and videos stay on your iPhone. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and we run no servers that receive your content.

What stays on your iPhone

Scripts you write or import, and the videos you record, are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. They are not uploaded to Little Works or to anyone else. We cannot see, access or recover them.

Camera and microphone

Teleprompter asks for camera and microphone access so it can show your camera preview and record your takes. Recording happens only after you press the record button. The words of your script are shown on screen for you to read and are never included in the video.

Photos

If you choose Save to Photos, the app asks for add-only access so it can save a copy of that take. It cannot see or read the rest of your photo library.

Documents you import

If you import a text, RTF or PDF document, it is read on your iPhone to create a script. The document is not sent anywhere.

Sharing

When you share a video, the iOS share sheet sends it to the destination you pick, such as Messages, Mail or Files. What happens there is governed by that service.

Purchases

Teleprompter Pro is a one-time in-app purchase processed by Apple through the App Store. Little Works never receives your payment details. The app asks Apple's StoreKit whether you own Pro; Apple's Privacy Policy covers that transaction.

Backups

Depending on your device settings, iCloud or computer backups made by Apple may include the app's data. Those backups are controlled by you and Apple, not by Little Works.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your scripts and takes remain on your iPhone until you delete them in the app or delete the app. Because we hold no copy of your data, there is nothing for us to delete or hand over. Save videos you want to keep to Photos or Files before removing the app.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking code, and it does not share data with data brokers or advertisers.

Children

Teleprompter is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Questions
