Common questions

Where are my videos saved?

Every take is kept on your iPhone in the Takes tab until you delete it. Recording again never replaces an earlier take. Tap a take, then Save to Photos or Share Video to keep a copy elsewhere.

Do the script words appear in my video?

No. The words are shown on screen only for you. The video contains your camera and microphone, nothing else.

How long can I record?

Free takes can be up to three minutes. The app stops and saves your take safely at the limit. Teleprompter Pro removes the limit.

The camera or microphone isn't working

Open the iPhone Settings app, find Teleprompter, and turn on Camera and Microphone. Then close and reopen the studio.

I bought Pro but it isn't unlocked

In Teleprompter, open Settings and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy Pro. Pro is a one-time purchase with no subscription.

A take says it needs attention

If recording was interrupted, for example by a call or the app closing, Teleprompter keeps the unfinished file. Open the Takes tab and choose Recover This Take. If the file can't be played, it stays in place until you delete it, and you can still share the original.

Will deleting the app delete my scripts and takes?

Yes. Everything is stored only on your iPhone, so save the videos you want to keep to Photos or Files first.
